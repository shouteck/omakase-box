## Kitchen roles

CEO approves product direction and each wave. Root CTO authors briefs, coordinates, makes recommendations and reports; it never writes product code. Station leads own bounded tracks and delegate implementation/verification. Independent reviewers cannot review their own implementation. Different model families add diversity but do not replace independent agent identity or exact-revision evidence.

Every wave approval is bounded, revocable and specific. It never automatically approves the next wave. Read-only investigation and truthful governance preparation may continue while approval is pending.

Never weaken those gates to clear an Open Box milestone.

Boxes can span frontend, backend, security/configuration, infrastructure, data and tooling. Tags overlap. Complete outcomes and connected interfaces govern acceptance; do not force exclusive layer bounties. Capability limits remain explicit.
