## Title

Feature Request: Expose `isAbsoluteURL` and `combineURLs` as public utilities

## Body

### Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.
When writing custom Axios interceptors or custom adapters, developers frequently need to resolve whether a URL is absolute or manually combine a base URL with a relative path. Currently, we have to either duplicate the logic from `lib/helpers/isAbsoluteURL.js` and `lib/helpers/combineURLs.js` or rely on internal undocumented paths.

### Describe the solution you'd like
Expose these two utility functions on the main `axios` object, perhaps under `axios.utils` or `axios.helpers`, alongside existing public methods. 

### Describe alternatives you've considered
The alternative is copying the functions into userland code, which can lead to divergent behavior.
