## Other attacks and limits

- **Exact state:** ephemeral runners and logs do not establish pending-search resume. The design correctly labels option 1 reconstructed visible history and installed import capability unverified. The separately approved no-inference check would prove serialization, not recover private reasoning. Option 2 needs actual filesystem checkpoints; real shared-prefix cost is correctly additional.
- **Repeats:** the one high-effort control repeat shows possible instability, not low-effort replay variance. Sixteen duplicate contexts cost 32 extra calls when each arm is repeated once. Keep those outside the main inference or freeze an inclusion rule. Shared active sessions, seeds or a changing backend can defeat independent generation. Deterministic responses conditional on contexts do not themselves defeat iid random corpus sampling. Repeats measure action instability, not task generalization.
- **Finite corpus:** equal-task-weight, with-replacement sampling justifies iid draws conditional on the corpus; repeated contexts are not new tasks/repositories. Gold-conditioned selection is development evidence. Retaining unchanged-order/no-room contexts and freezing reconstruction exclusions avoids selection after outcomes.
- **Stopping:** Hoeffding range-two radii, .05/3 interval allocation, clipping, three fixed looks and union bound are sound. At zero, .193397 + .112133 < .35 gives the final equivalence guarantee. Correct labels/instrument and iid draws are assumptions; statistical 5% does not cover their violation. Option 3's roughly 80-point success guarantee is honest, and normal log-ratio planning is labelled uncalibrated.
- **Costs:** amounts multiply lagged boundary proxies, not cost prediction intervals. Usage lag, closing responses, overlapping windows, long imported histories and extra real prefixes are disclosed. Reported tokens include cached input/reasoning. Actual cost may exceed envelopes: funded cap and undecided stop are required. No cheaper-model saving is established. The .551 repeat SD is a single scenario, not an estimated population SD.
- **Native gate:** fresh held-out pipeline counterexamples and stdout/stderr/status/side-effect checks directly target the observed fault. Sorting upstream before early downstream close cannot preserve general native behaviour. Post-pipeline transformation remains an engineering constraint to prove. A finite suite is not universal equivalence. No agent tokens while that gate fails.

No novel method is claimed. Local runner sources support the access limitation; I did not probe the installed server or independently establish the cited online API behaviour.
