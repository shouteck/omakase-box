## Cheapest confident wrong answer

A change of claim costs nothing: turn more gold-function opens into a claim of cheaper repairs, or many draws from familiar repositories into confidence on new tasks. Missing private reasoning can change a replay; a broad file read can open the gold function without locating the cause; a promoted non-fix function can be a detour or a necessary dependency. None establishes repair savings. Frozen explicit-open scoring, unchanged-order denominator and conditional-corpus wording prevent those errors if enforced. Missing state and native interception faults are validity gates, not uncertainties cured by more samples.

## Required corrections

1. **Use the category structure and show noise sensitivity.** D is in {-1,0,1}. Win/loss counts each have Binomial(n,p) marginals under the proposed iid design. Exact one-sided bounds give a difference interval [Lwin-Uloss, Uwin-Lloss]. Four tails at each of three looks, each alpha .05/12, give simultaneous 5% error control by union bound, despite dependent counts. With zero discordant pairs at n128, the interval is ±[1-(.05/12)^(1/128)] = ±.041914: five-point equivalence is possible at that cost. At n64/n256 the radii are .082071/.021181. This is conditional precision, not a forecast of low discordance or power. Preregister replacement exact inference, or share the error allocation if retaining/intersecting Hoeffding too. Show sparse/high-discordance scenarios and the undecided budget outcome. Describe 9,559 as a conservative sufficient count for unknown-noise directional power, not a necessary size for every five-point answer.

2. **Match power to the stopping rule.** Radius plus sqrt(2 log(5)/n) proves 80% directional rejection at the fixed look if reached and analysed. Earlier equivalence can stop the protocol. At true final threshold .305529, n128 equivalence is compatible with the true ±.35 claim and can prevent the final helps label. The range-only bound permits .034832 extra early-equivalence probability; the simple proof therefore does not establish 80% final helps probability. This is a proof/wording gap, not a demonstrated actual counterexample to 80% power. At the specified looks help and equivalence cannot simultaneously qualify: twice the final radius is .386793 > .35, making the priority clause inert. State fixed-look power, or assess complete terminal-outcome power. If tightening inference/margin, recalculate the stopping/power statements together.

## Other attacks and limits

- **Exact state:** ephemeral runners and logs do not establish pending-search resume. The design correctly labels option 1 reconstructed visible history and installed import capability unverified. The separately approved no-inference check would prove serialization, not recover private reasoning. Option 2 needs actual filesystem checkpoints; real shared-prefix cost is correctly additional.
- **Repeats:** the one high-effort control repeat shows possible instability, not low-effort replay variance. Sixteen duplicate contexts cost 32 extra calls when each arm is repeated once. Keep those outside the main inference or freeze an inclusion rule. Shared active sessions, seeds or a changing backend can defeat independent generation. Deterministic responses conditional on contexts do not themselves defeat iid random corpus sampling. Repeats measure action instability, not task generalization.
- **Finite corpus:** equal-task-weight, with-replacement sampling justifies iid draws conditional on the corpus; repeated contexts are not new tasks/repositories. Gold-conditioned selection is development evidence. Retaining unchanged-order/no-room contexts and freezing reconstruction exclusions avoids selection after outcomes.
- **Stopping:** Hoeffding range-two radii, .05/3 interval allocation, clipping, three fixed looks and union bound are sound. At zero, .193397 + .112133 < .35 gives the final equivalence guarantee. Correct labels/instrument and iid draws are assumptions; statistical 5% does not cover their violation. Option 3's roughly 80-point success guarantee is honest, and normal log-ratio planning is labelled uncalibrated.
- **Costs:** amounts multiply lagged boundary proxies, not cost prediction intervals. Usage lag, closing responses, overlapping windows, long imported histories and extra real prefixes are disclosed. Reported tokens include cached input/reasoning. Actual cost may exceed envelopes: funded cap and undecided stop are required. No cheaper-model saving is established. The .551 repeat SD is a single scenario, not an estimated population SD.
- **Native gate:** fresh held-out pipeline counterexamples and stdout/stderr/status/side-effect checks directly target the observed fault. Sorting upstream before early downstream close cannot preserve general native behaviour. Post-pipeline transformation remains an engineering constraint to prove. A finite suite is not universal equivalence. No agent tokens while that gate fails.

No novel method is claimed. Local runner sources support the access limitation; I did not probe the installed server or independently establish the cited online API behaviour. The design does not use that citation to assert installed capability. An eventual runtime-specific claim still needs the proposed non-runtime ranking baseline.
