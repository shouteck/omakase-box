## Limitations and blockers

- The ranker model covers exactly the SPEC demonstration envelope (0..4 items, a..d, integer scores 0..2). Larger sizes, other score domains, other ID alphabets and timing/performance behaviour are outside the model, refused by it, and recorded only as unknowns; a deployment may register additional bound sets in CALLER_BOUND_SETS for other adapters.
- Caller-assumption checking is declaration-based classification plus one bounded behavioural verification hook; semantic interpretation of caller code is neither attempted nor claimed.
