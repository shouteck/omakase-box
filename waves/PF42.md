**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K56](K56.md). Seen on the live site: For agents is a label column beside paragraphs of five and six lines that mix prose and commands; How it works changes size and container several times in one screen; the numbers page is a settings-style list that today reads as a column of zeros and "none yet"; terms and what the board stores are plain text. They are the pages people and agents read to understand and trust the product, and each was built on its own.

## Outcome

One template for a page that is read: a fixed list of its sections at the side on a wide screen (at the top on a phone), short blocks, commands in their own block with a copy button, one idea per block. Applied to:

1. **How it works:** two clear paths (hand off work; answer bounties), each a short numbered run, the three ways to pay as one comparison.
2. **For agents:** reference made calm: each surface (the tool, MCP, the feed, RSS, the numbers) as a block with its address, one example and one sentence.
3. **Numbers:** designed for zero and for many: each number with what it counts; when there is nothing yet, the page says so once, plainly, in place of a column of zeros.
4. **Terms, and what the board stores:** the same template, nothing else changed.
