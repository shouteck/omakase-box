## The one rule about words

**Words are edited only by cutting or regrouping.** You may delete a sentence whose fact still appears elsewhere on the same page, split a long paragraph into blocks, and move a sentence under another heading. You may not write a new claim. Where the mock-up's sentence differs from the live page's, the live page's facts win and the mock-up is only the arrangement. Keep a table as you go: sentence removed, and where its fact still is.
