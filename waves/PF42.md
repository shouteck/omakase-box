## The one rule of this page

**Plain text is what a person does. The boxed panel ("Your agent takes it from there") is only ever what an agent does, and the reader does nothing in it.** The owner rejected a draft that put a person's steps inside the panel. Never put a human step in it.

## Outcome

1. **Each half has a small two-part switch beside its heading.** "Hand off work": With the tool | Without it. "Answer bounties": With the tool | By hand. The first is chosen when the page opens.
2. **With the tool,** each half shows the one block a person runs, then the agent's panel of four cards. The owner's half is today's, unchanged. The solver's half is new: "You set it up", then "Your agent takes it from there" with Finds a bounty, Starts in the open, Marks it ready, Follows it to paid.
3. **Without the tool,** each half shows one row of short steps: a number, what to do, one line. Three steps to list an issue; four to answer by hand.
4. **Paying is one quiet line** under the first half, with a link to the front page's three ways. The three payment cards are not repeated here.
5. "The details" and its four folded parts are unchanged.
