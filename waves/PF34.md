# PF34: the board's bounty detail answers inside the tool's limit

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: evidence · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF34` · Timebox: 120 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-04-pf9-split
Horizon: long by concept (a cause not yet known): start with `/plan` and keep the plan in this file

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K48](K48.md). [PF26](PF26.md) found the live board's bounty detail answering in 0.4 to 9 seconds against the tool's 2.5-second limit (`board.ts` `DEADLINE_MS`), so a solver's `status` often says "board: not read (it took too long)". The Worker showed 13 to 20 ms of CPU and 4 to 6 s of wall time; it was not the GitHub re-check. Never investigated. Bug-fix playbook: reproduce, then the root cause, then the smallest fix.

## Outcome

The cause of the wait is named from a measurement, and a bounty's detail on the deployed board answers under 1 second at the median and under 2.5 seconds at the slowest of 30 reads, or the Result says which part cannot be made faster on the free plan and what the tool tells a solver instead.

## Scope

- Write: `packages/box-platform/src/feed.ts`, `query.ts`, `payments.ts` or `store.ts` as the cause requires; `tests/platform/`; `packages/box-bounty/board.ts` only if the answer is a different limit, with its reason
- Read for context: `bountyDetail` in `feed.ts` (it awaits about seven reads one after another: the bounty, the owner's records, payments, paying, weights, facts, the author's Stripe state), [PF28](PF28.md), [PF8](PF8.md)'s CPU numbers, `DEPLOY.md`
- Do not touch: what the detail says; the feed's schema; anything a signed-in page shows

## Done when

- `node scripts/verify.mjs test:platform test:box-bounty test:kitchen` exits 0 on the final commit.
- **Reproduced before any change.** The live board is empty and never lists the sandbox, so say in the plan how: time each of the detail's reads on the deployed database with `wrangler tail` on a bounty that exists there, or, if none exists, on a local board with the same data shape, stating that a local run cannot show Cloudflare's own waits. If it cannot be reproduced, report that and stop; do not fix a guess.
- The measurement is 30 reads spread over at least 15 minutes, cold and warm named apart, with median, slowest and what limits it.
- A test fails if the detail makes more database round trips than the number the fix settles on.
- A deploy is the owner's word; `exceededCpu` before and after, as every PF close records.
