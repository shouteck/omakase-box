**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K56](K56.md). The front page is the site's best page and its direction stays. Three things keep it from finished, seen on the live site that day:

- **On a phone the hero's picture first appears as a tall empty black box,** because the terminal draws its story after the page loads.
- **A section can be absent until a script reveals it.** One capture of a page mid-scroll showed most of a screen blank.
- **The install line is a click away.** Developer tools put the command itself in the hero with a copy button; ours is behind "Get started".

It also has to carry what the coming waves change: every bounty pays ([BT60](BT60.md)), a maintainer can list an issue with no install ([PF32](PF32.md)), and the card is redrawn ([PF41](PF41.md)).

## Outcome

1. **The picture is complete at first paint,** at every width and with scripts off: the finished state is what loads; motion, where allowed, plays over a page that is already whole.
2. **Nothing on the site is hidden until a script runs.** A section may move as it arrives; it is never invisible while it waits.
3. **The install line is in the hero,** copyable, with "Browse bounties" beside it. One primary action stays.
4. **Both doors are shown:** the agent's path and the maintainer's path, as the page's two ways in, without adding a screen.
5. **The picture uses the real card** from PF41 and a reward that could be real.
