## Outcome

1. **The install line sits under the two buttons in the hero,** in a pill, with a copy button. "Get started" and "Browse bounties" stay above it.
2. **A third way in:** "Maintain a repository?" joins "Have an agent?" and "Want to solve?" at the foot of the page, between them, in the same tile. Three across on a wide screen, stacked on a phone.
3. **Nothing on the site is invisible while it waits to move.** Today each line of the picture is hidden until its turn (up to four seconds), so a slow phone first shows an empty dark box, and a section can be blank until it scrolls into view. The motion stays; the hiding goes.
