## Outcome

`take` records an expected-by time (a lease) on the attempt. The issue, board and `status` say “taken by X, expected by <time>”, then “<n> h past expected-by”. These are facts, not verdicts. The same solver can renew by re-running `take`; nothing expires the attempt, releases it, blocks another solver or sends a reminder.

## Changed expectations

`take.ts` currently refuses an already-taken open attempt as `already-taken`; same-solver re-take will renew instead. List exact existing test names here before changing them. Other already-taken cases and submit/withdraw semantics stay intact; legacy attempts gain “no expected-by”.

## Traps

- A lease is an expectation, never exclusivity, authority to run foreign code or a judgment of the solver.
- Do not infer solver identity or time from free text. No scheduler, owner reminder or Board dependency.
