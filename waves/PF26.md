# PF26: `submit`, and the solver's `status` to merged and paid

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF26` · Timebox: 180 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-03-solver-in-the-tool, D-2026-11-03-cli-and-board-split
Horizon: short, with a real-GitHub step in the sandbox. Feature playbook; principles: experience-first, prove-it-works

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 3 November 2026, replacing part of PF12 (see [SOLVER-CLI.md](../docs/box-v4/SOLVER-CLI.md)). Needs PF25.

## Outcome

A solver agent finishes the work, runs `box bounty submit`, and follows its attempt to the end with `status`:
- the poster's review comments, marked as theirs;
- merged or closed;
- paid or not, and how.

If no way to be paid is connected, `status` names the board's link once. That is the one human step.

## Scope

- Write: `packages/box-bounty/` (a new `submit`, `status` for taken bounties, help), `tests/box/bounty/`.
- Do not touch: the board (beyond reading it), payments, the posting commands' behaviour.

## Build

1. **`submit`:** marks the draft ready for review, with the bounty format's line. It refuses if the branch has no change beyond the empty commit. The record moves to submitted.
2. **`status` on taken and submitted bounties:**
   - the pull request's state;
   - review comments by others, marked as theirs (as `--comments` does today);
   - merged or closed;
   - from the board, whether it is paid and how (pay on merge, one click, recorded by the owner).
3. **The payment step:** when the board says the solver has no way to be paid connected, `status` prints one line with the board's link, like PF20's pay-on-merge line. The tool cannot connect it. No reminders after that (D-2026-11-02-no-owed-reminders).

## Done when

- `node scripts/verify.mjs test:box-bounty test:kitchen` exits 0 on the final commit.
- **Real GitHub, in the sandbox only:** a taken bounty goes through submit, a review comment, a merge by the poster's tool, and `status` shows it merged.
- **Paid state:** a test fails if the paid state is made fixed. Each state is read from a stand-in board.
- **Hostile text:** a review comment is printed as the commenter's words, never as the tool's.

## Lenses

- [x] **Security:** hostile review comments; a pull request renamed or retargeted by someone else.
- [x] **User experience:** a fresh agent follows its attempt to merged without being told how.
- [ ] **Measurement fairness:** not applicable.
