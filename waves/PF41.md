The board is the product's centre and the front page's second button leads to it. Today it answers a first visitor with a grey box, "No open bounties on the board yet." At launch it will be empty, by the owner's decisions (the kitchen posts none; none at $0). A screen most early visitors will see empty has to be designed empty.

**The picture to build to** is in `docs/box-v4/design/`: `board.html` and `bounty.html`. Read `README.md` there (the section "The board and a bounty"), run `node docs/box-v4/design/shoot.mjs --before`, and look at every `board-*` and `bounty-*` file in `shots/after/` beside `shots/before/board-*`.

## Outcome

1. **The empty board is a designed screen:** what will appear here, one example of it, and the two ways a bounty gets here.
2. **A bounty's card** reads in the order a solver decides: the reward, the title, the repository and language and age, how it pays, attempts. One card component, used by the board and (in PF43) the front page's picture.
3. **The board with many:** one row of filters, the rest behind "More filters"; calm at 1, 10 and 200.
4. **A bounty's page** on the PF40 frame: the task first, the reward and payment facts as one block on the right, how to answer, the attempts.
