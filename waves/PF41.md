# PF41: the board and a bounty, designed for empty, one and many

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF41` · Timebox: 300 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval, D-2026-11-02-design-references-big-companies, D-2026-10-21-minimalism, D-2026-11-10-no-zero-bounties, D-2026-11-04-dual-usage

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K56](K56.md). The board is the product's centre and the front page's second button leads to it. Today it answers a first visitor with a grey box, "No open bounties on the board yet." At launch it will be empty, by the owner's decisions (the kitchen posts none; none at $0). The numbers of zero bounties will hold for a while. A screen that will be seen empty by most early visitors has to be designed empty.

## Outcome

1. **The empty board is a designed screen:** it says what will appear here, shows it, and offers the two ways a bounty gets here (an agent posts one with the tool; a maintainer puts a reward on an existing issue). Whether it shows an example bounty is the owner's answer to K56's question 2; both versions are in the mock-ups.
2. **A bounty's card** reads at a glance in the order a solver decides: the reward, the title, the repository and language, how it pays, attempts in progress, age. One card design for the board, the front page's picture and a bounty's page header.
3. **The board with many:** filters and search that stay calm at 1, 10 and 200 bounties; the reward as the strongest element after the title (every bounty pays, after [BT60](BT60.md)).
4. **A bounty's page** on the PF40 frame: the task first, the reward and payment facts as one block ([PF31](PF31.md)'s three lines kept), how to answer, the attempts.
