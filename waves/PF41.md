# PF41: the empty board says what will be here and how one gets here

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF41` · Timebox: 90 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-11-design-approved, D-2026-11-11-site-settled, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval

**Brief.** Rewritten by the CTO on 11 November 2026 from the design the owner approved that day. An earlier, larger version (a new card, new filters, a new bounty page) was rejected and is gone: **the board with bounties and a bounty's page stay exactly as they are.** This wave changes one thing: what the board shows when it lists nothing. At launch that is what most visitors will see.

**The approved picture is made by a script.** Run the local board (`node packages/box-platform/local.mjs`), then `node docs/box-v4/design/review.mjs`, and open `docs/box-v4/design/review/board-empty-desktop-after.jpg` and `board-empty-phone-after.jpg`. The exact markup is the function `emptyBoard` in `docs/box-v4/design/review.mjs`: it is the design.

## Outcome

When the board lists no bounty, in place of today's grey box:

1. A centred message: **"No open bounties yet."** and the sentence "A bounty appears here a few minutes after someone posts one on a public GitHub repository."
2. Under it, **two plain blue links** side by side: "Post one with your agent ›" to `/how/`, and "List an issue you already have ›" to `/me/`.
3. Below, at most 46rem wide and centred: the small label "What one will look like", **one sample row** drawn exactly like a real row at 70% opacity, and under it "An example. Every name in it is invented."

## Scope

- Read for context: `docs/box-v4/design/README.md`, `docs/box-v4/design/review.mjs` (`emptyBoard`)
- Do not touch: `Card`, `RecordLine`, the filters, the list, paging, `components/BountyView.jsx`, `src/`, the feed

## Steps, in order

1. **Make and look at the approved pictures** (above), and at `board-empty-desktop-before.jpg` beside them.
2. **Tests first** (the table below). The page tests seed bounties; for an empty board start a rig with no bounty seeded, or intercept `/api/board` in the browser and return its real answer with `open_total: 0`, `count: 0`, `bounties: []` (that is how `review.mjs` does it: copy its `page.route`).
3. **The block,** in `Board.jsx`. Find `data.open_total === 0 ? (` and replace only the `<div className="border border-line bg-surface …">…</div>` that follows with:
   - a `div` with a top hairline (`border-t border-line`), generous padding (4.5rem above, 3.5rem below), centred text, `aria-live="polite"` kept;
   - `<p className="text-xl font-medium tracking-tight">No open bounties yet.</p>`;
   - the sentence in `text-soft`, at most 30rem wide, centred;
   - a row (`flex flex-wrap justify-center`, gaps 0.5rem by 2rem) holding the two links, each `font-medium` with no underline and a `›` in `<span aria-hidden="true">`, exactly as the front page writes "Browse bounties ›";
   - then a `div` at most 46rem wide, centred, with `data-example`: the label, a `<ul>` holding one `<Card b={EXAMPLE} />`, and the closing sentence.
4. **The sample is a constant in the file, never fetched and never stored:**

   ```js
   const EXAMPLE = { id: 'example', title: 'Recurring transactions skip the 31st', repository: 'example-org/ledger', language: 'TypeScript', age_days: 2, solutions_open: 2, amount: 45, poster: 'example-org',
     record: { has_history: true, merged: 4, recorded_paid: 4, confirmed_received: 3, says_not_received: 0, owed_now: 0, oldest_owed_days: 0 } };
   ```

   Render it through the real `Card`, so it can never drift from a real row. The real `Card` links its title to `/b/<id>/`: give `Card` one optional prop, `example`, that renders the title as plain text (a `<p>`) and leaves everything else alone. Wrap the `<ul>` in a `div` with `style={{ opacity: 0.7 }}`.
5. **Look at it** on the local board with the answer forced empty, at 1440 and 390, light and dark.
6. **Built beside approved:** your build next to the pictures from step 1; any difference is fixed or listed.
7. **Verify and merge.** Then stop: deploying is the owner's word; `exceededCpu` before and after.

## Tests to write first

| Test | It fails when |
|---|---|
| With an empty answer, `/board/` shows `No open bounties yet.`, a link to `/how/` and a link to `/me/` inside `main` | a way in is missing |
| `[data-example]` contains the words `An example. Every name in it is invented.` and no `a[href^="/b/"]` | the sample looks real, or links to a bounty that does not exist |
| With the same empty answer, `/bounties.json`, `/numbers.json`, `/rss.xml` and the MCP `find_bounties` answer contain no `example-org/ledger` | the sample leaks into data |
| With bounties seeded, the page has no `[data-example]` and no `No open bounties yet.` | the sample shows on a board with bounties |
| With filters that match nothing on a board that has bounties, the page still says `No open bounty matches these filters.` | the two empty cases were merged |
| At 390 wide `scrollWidth <= 390` | the row overflows |
