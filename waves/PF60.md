# PF60: "paid" is three facts, each with one writer, and the owner's hand payment is recorded by the tool

Milestone: platform, with a tool part · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch`, after [PF59](PF59.md) and [BT89](BT89.md) · Branch: `wave/PF60` · Timebox: 180 minutes (a hosted run takes about 25); at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-24-paid-is-three-facts, D-2026-11-24-one-fact-one-owner, D-2026-11-24-loosening-needs-the-owner
Horizon: short to medium. Refactoring playbook; principles: subtract-before-you-add, prove-it-works.

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 24 November 2026. The owner first chose the board as the only "paid" ledger. They then pointed out that this puts a person on a website for what the agent should do through the tool, and approved this split instead ("yes"). D-2026-11-24-paid-is-three-facts supersedes D-2026-11-24-board-is-the-paid-ledger.

**Why.** "Paid" is written in two places today:
- **in the tool:** `omakase-box config set paid <id>`, `recordPaid` in `packages/box-bounty/owner.ts`, which writes a `paid` event locally and on the shared memory (`sharePaid`, `chain.ts`), and sends the board a hint;
- **on the board:** the bounty's page, signed in (`recordPaid` in `packages/box-platform/src/account.ts`).

Two writers for one fact break the owner's rule (D-2026-11-24-one-fact-one-owner). But "paid" is really three facts, and each has a natural owner:

| Fact | Its one writer |
|---|---|
| the board charged the owner's card (pay on merge, one click) | the board, from Stripe's and PayPal's own confirmations |
| the owner says they paid by hand | **the tool** (`config set paid`, run by the agent), on the shared memory, which the board already reads (`readEvents`) |
| the solver says received or not received | the board, signed in as the solver (`answerReceipt`) |

So the duplicate is the board's own "record paid by hand" for bounties posted with the tool.

## Outcome

1. **For a bounty posted with the tool, the board shows the tool's record and does not take its own.**
   - The bounty's page shows "the owner recorded paying by hand on `<day>`" from the chain event.
   - Its record-paid action answers 409: "this bounty's hand payment is recorded by the owner's tool: omakase-box config set paid `<id>`; nothing changed".
   - A bounty listed from the board (PF32, held under [PF57](PF57.md)) has no shared memory, so its record-paid action stays as it is: one writer for that kind of bounty.
2. **`config set paid <id>` stays the agent's way to record a hand payment,** with no confirmation needed: it only reduces what is owed, and [BT88](BT88.md) logs it. It sends the board its hint as today, so the board reads the record before its next charge check.
3. **Withdrawing a paid record is loosening.** `config set paid <id> --withdraw`, or the spelling BT89 settles on, makes the bounty owed again, which can let pay on merge charge the owner's card. It asks the owner's confirmation in the terminal, or on the board after [BT90](BT90.md) (D-2026-11-24-loosening-needs-the-owner). BT89's table marks it so.
4. **The tool shows all three facts, each from its owner.** `status`, the overview and the pay message:
   - take "charged" and "the solver says received / not received" from the board's public fact (`readBountyFact`);
   - take "the owner says paid by hand" from the tool's own record.

   When the board cannot be read, its two facts say "unknown: the board could not be read", never a guess.
5. **Every description of recording a payment says the same:** record a hand payment with the tool; charges and the solver's word are the board's. That covers the pay message, BOUNTY.md, FIRST-FIVE-MINUTES.md, `describe`, the skill, `CLAIMS.md` (BT92) and `OWNER-SURFACE.md` (BT89's rows: three facts, three owners).
