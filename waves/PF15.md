## Why

Each way to pay arrived in its own wave, and nothing explains them together. Read on master on 2 November 2026:

| Reader | Sees today | Wrong or missing |
|---|---|---|
| Solver, in a new issue | `pay-to:` with a Stripe payment link is the way to ask; the poster pays by hand | Connected accounts and every other method |
| Owner's agent, after a merge | "which you pay by hand", plus a note on pay on merge | One click, PayPal |
| Owner's agent, `status` | A Stripe payment as Stripe's word | From reading `board.ts`, a PayPal payment is not recognised as PayPal's word: confirm by test |
| Agent, `describe` | Nothing on how a reward is paid | That there are three ways |
| Feed and MCP | `reward.paid` has two answers; `author_takes_paypal` has no Stripe partner; `MONEY` and `get_bounty`'s description omit one click | One statement of how this bounty pays |
| Solver, in `how to answer` | List your ways; Stripe only on a pay-on-merge bounty | That connecting makes payment easier |
| Visitor, front page | One tile: by hand and pay on merge | One click |
| Visitor, How it works | Step 7 "You pay, and record it"; "How payment works" | One click, PayPal, any comparison |

## Outcome

Each reader learns, at the moment it matters and no sooner, that there are three ways to pay, and which apply to them. The words are the same everywhere and say what is true on this board: a way with no key says "not set up on this board yet".

| | By hand | One click | On merge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Through** | Wise, wallet, any listed way | Stripe or PayPal | Stripe |
| **Solver needs** | To list a way | A connected account | Connected Stripe |
| **After the merge, the owner** | Pays, then records it | Clicks and pays on the provider's page | Does nothing, after saving a card and limit once |
| **Who says it was paid** | The owner | The provider | The provider |

Say "three ways", never "levels": by hand reaches the most people. Monid's balance appears nowhere, because it does not exist.

Keep PF10's visual system: no new colours, fonts, sizes or panels without the owner's word. Merge after the owner approves, and revise until they do. A production deploy needs its own word.
