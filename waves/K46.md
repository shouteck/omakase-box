# K46: pay on merge, proven in Stripe's test mode at $0, or the blocker named

Milestone: K (kitchen) · Station: evidence · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/K46` · Timebox: 300 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-01-pay-on-merge, D-2026-11-01-agent-only-direction

**Brief.** Rewritten by the CTO on 1 November 2026, from the owner's agreement in talk. **Runs before PF9.** A proof, not a build: nothing goes into the product, and no real money moves. **Stops once for the owner** to open a Stripe account, which no agent may do. Legal review is not a gate (owner).

## Why

A person who must remember to pay is the flow's weakest step, and the tool must not nag anyone about it. Raw payment links also look unprofessional and verify nothing. Established tools solve this differently: [Calendly](https://calendly.com/help/payments-faq) connects a person's own Stripe or PayPal and holds nothing, and [Algora](https://docs.algora.io/bounties/payments) can pay automatically on merge.

## The design to prove

1. The solver clicks "Connect Stripe" once.
2. The owner saves a card once and sets a limit: the reward limit the tool already has.
3. `apply` merges, and a direct charge pays the solver's own Stripe account. "Ask me each time" stays the default; automatic payment is what the owner turns on.
4. Stripe tells the board it was paid.

The platform never holds money and takes no fee. This replaces K42's design, which used destination charges and made the platform liable.

## The $0 rule

The platform pays nothing, in the test or afterwards. Use Connect's ["Stripe handles pricing"](https://stripe.com/connect/pricing) option, with [fees collected from the connected account](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-fee-payer-behavior), and negative balances with Stripe. Confirm each on the day: the pricing page read here is old. **If any step needs money from the owner, including a paid business registration, it is a named blocker. Stop and report it; do not work around it or pay.**

## Questions the proof answers

- **Reach:** can a Singapore platform make direct charges to solvers' accounts abroad? K42b's 37 countries were for a different setup. List what is confirmed, what is refused and what is unknown.
- **The saved card:** can the owner's card, saved once with the platform, be charged off-session on a direct charge to a solver's account? This is the core of pay on merge.
- **Real cards:** what happens when a card asks for extra authentication at merge, with nobody there? Name the resulting state and its next step.
- **Solvers:** can an individual connect, with what checks? Say what a solver without Stripe does: they use plain payment details and the owner pays by hand.
- **The owner:** does Stripe let an individual in Singapore run this platform, or does it need a registered business? Which fees does the solver see, and how much of a $45 reward arrives?
- **Failures:** a declined card, a limit reached, a duplicate `apply`, a solver who disconnected, and a refund or dispute. Each ends in a named state, and none charges twice.

## How it runs

1. **Read first:** `docs/box-v4/PLATFORM.md` and `PLATFORM-MONEY-SG.md`, then Stripe's Connect pages on the day. Note what still holds.
2. **The owner's stop:** the owner opens a free Stripe account, turns on Connect in test mode and sets a test key in a terminal environment variable. The key never enters a chat, a file or a commit. No card, payment or business detail is needed for test mode; if Stripe asks, that is a finding.
3. **The bench:** a throwaway script outside the repository, in `%TEMP%\kitchen-K46-*`, on Stripe's test mode with its published test cards and test connected accounts. Save a card, connect two test solvers, and run the design and every failure above.
4. **The report:** `docs/box-v4/PAY-ON-MERGE.md`. Its first page is the owner's: whether it works, what the owner sets up, what a solver does, what it costs each side, and what could go wrong.

The front page's "Touch your money" and "carries no money" lines must stay true once the site can start a charge.

## Plan

Approved by the owner on 1 November 2026.

1. **Bench:** one dependency-free Node script in `%TEMP%\kitchen-K46-bench`. It reads the key only from `STRIPE_TEST_KEY`, refuses anything not `sk_test_`, and redacts the key and client secrets from everything it writes.
2. **Owner's stop:** a free Stripe account, Connect on in test mode, the key in an environment variable. Anything else Stripe asks for is a finding; a request for money is a named blocker.
3. **Run, one scenario at a time:** reach (SG, US, DE, JP, AU, GB, TH, AE, IN, BR, with a real off-session direct charge per country that activates); paid merge; fees on $45 and the platform's balance before and after; over the limit; duplicate `apply`; authentication at merge; declined card, insufficient funds, disconnected solver, refund, dispute; wrong payee; the connected account's events. Live webhook delivery stays unproven: the bench has no public address.
4. **Report:** `docs/box-v4/PAY-ON-MERGE.md`, with the owner's page first, an answer to every question, the redacted transcript, the platform's cost as a number and the PF11 outline.
5. **Close:** diff empty for `packages` and `tests`, no key in the commit, verify, merge, STATUS, scratch removed. The owner revokes the test key.
