# PF59: the card is the board's only owner setting: one maximum per bounty, no per-repository switches, never told to pay twice

Milestone: platform, with a tool part · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch`, after [PF55](PF55.md) · Branch: `wave/PF59` · Timebox: 300 minutes (a hosted run takes about 25); at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-24-card-is-the-only-board-setting, D-2026-11-24-one-fact-one-owner, D-2026-11-01-pay-on-merge, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval
Horizon: medium to long: a money rule and a live-database migration; starts with `/plan`. Refactoring playbook; principles: subtract-before-you-add, fix-root-causes, prove-it-works.

**Brief.** Rewritten by the CTO on 24 November 2026. The owner noted that values set on the board change how the tool's flow runs, and asked for this to be "correctly handled". They then decided that the board must hold no such settings: the only owner friction on the board is saving the card, plus one maximum on the same screen ("yes agreed"; D-2026-11-24-card-is-the-only-board-setting).

**Why.** Pay on merge now depends on values set in two places:
- **the tool's reward:** `allow --reward` and `config set reward`, bound into each bounty at post;
- **the board's per-repository switch and amount:** `savePay` in `packages/box-platform/src/account.ts`; `pay_repositories`, `pay_settings.limit_per_bounty`.

`decide` (`src/charge.ts`) charges only when the two are equal. So:
- **A change on one side silently stops charges.** Raise the reward in the tool, and the board never charges.
- **The board's "limit" clashes with the tool's `reward-limit`:** same word, different things.
- **At close the tool cannot see the switch,** so it gives hand-pay instructions even when the board is about to charge (`payNotice`, `packages/box-bounty/pay.ts`). An owner who pays outside the board without recording it pays twice.

The owner's decision removes the cause: the tool owns which repositories, the reward and whether to ask, and the board holds only the card.

## Outcome

1. **Saving a card, with its maximum, is the consent to pay on merge.**
   - The owner's page has one step: save a card, and type **the most the card pays per bounty** (whole dollars, at least the $1 minimum). It says in plain words: "your card pays each of your merged bounties its reward automatically, up to $N per bounty; above that, nothing is charged and you are told".
   - Removing the card turns pay on merge off.
   - Changing the maximum is the same one field.
   - There is no per-repository switch and no per-repository amount.
2. **The charge rule** (`decide`). A merged bounty is charged when:
   - it was posted by the card owner's GitHub account (`poster_id`), with the tool (a chain exists; listed bounties keep their own rule);
   - it carries its reward as bound at post (PF55's `posted_amount`, which must equal the issue's figure);
   - that reward is **at most** the card's maximum;
   - the merge came after the card and its maximum were saved (the existing "never charge an old merge" rule, keyed on that time);
   - no hand payment is recorded (`facts.recorded`, PF60).

   Above the maximum it settles `refused-over-limit`, worded "above the most your card pays per bounty ($N)". Every other rule of the charge is unchanged.
3. **The tool and the board agree, and the owner is never told to pay twice.** The tool reads the bounty's public `pays_on_merge`, which now means "the poster saved a card whose maximum covers this reward, and the bounty was posted with the tool":
   - **At close, after a merge:** when it applies, the pay message says "the board will charge your saved card for $X; do not also pay by hand. omakase-box status `<id>` shows the charge", with no hand-pay instruction. When it does not apply, the message is today's. When the board cannot be read, the message says so and asks the owner to check the bounty's page before paying by hand.
   - **At `status` after a post:** "pay on merge: applies" or "does not apply to this bounty". When it does not apply and the reward is above any card maximum, the words name the page: `<board>/me/`.
   - **At `allow --reward` and `config set reward`, with a board set:** one line, "if you saved a card on the board, it pays automatically only up to its maximum per bounty: `<board>/me/`".
4. **The data moves without charging anyone by surprise.** A migration, `packages/box-platform/migrations/pf59-*.sql`:
   - drops `pay_repositories`;
   - keeps `pay_settings` (the card) and gives it `max_per_bounty` and `max_set_at`;
   - for an owner who had switches on, sets the maximum to the highest per-repository amount they had approved, and `max_set_at` to the earliest switch-on time, so nothing that was not chargeable before becomes chargeable.

   The owner applies it at deploy (`packages/box-platform/DEPLOY.md`). An owner with a card and no switches gets no maximum, and no automatic charge until they set one: fail safe.
5. **The words are true everywhere:**
   - the owner's page;
   - `src/feed.ts`'s `pays_on_merge` description and the schema;
   - `docs/box-v4/PAY-ON-MERGE.md`;
   - `docs/box-v4/OWNER-SURFACE.md` (BT89's board rows: the card and its maximum, and the solver's own accounts);
   - `CLAIMS.md` (BT92);
   - `describe`'s money line.

## Traps

- **The migration is the risk.** Never widen what is chargeable: an owner's new maximum is the highest amount they had already approved, dated at their earliest switch-on. Test it on a copy; the owner applies it to the live database.
- **The maximum is the one cap an agent cannot reach.** It is set only on the signed-in page, never by the tool and never by an API the tool calls.
- **`pays_on_merge` is the board's word "as of" its last read,** so the tool's words say "the board says".
- **The approved maximum is private.** The tool shows only the public yes or no, and never asks for or prints the amount.
