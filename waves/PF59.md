# PF59: the tool and the board agree on whether a bounty is charged on merge, and the owner is never told to pay twice

Milestone: platform, with a tool part · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch`, after [PF55](PF55.md) · Branch: `wave/PF59` · Timebox: 240 minutes (a hosted run takes about 25); at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-01-pay-on-merge, D-2026-11-01-pay-switch-on-board, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval, D-2026-11-24-charge-up-to-ceiling, D-2026-11-24-one-fact-one-owner
Horizon: medium. Bug fix playbook; principles: fix-root-causes, prove-it-works.

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 24 November 2026. The owner noted that values set on the board change how the tool's flow runs, and asked that this be "correctly handled".

**Why.** Pay on merge depends on two values, set in two places, which the owner must keep equal by hand:
- **the tool's reward:** `allow --reward` and `config set reward`, written into each bounty's format block at post;
- **the board's amount per bounty:** set on the owner's page, per repository (`savePay`, `packages/box-platform/src/account.ts`; `pay_repositories.limit_per_bounty`).

The board charges only when they are equal (`decide`, `src/charge.ts`: `facts.amount !== card.limit` settles `refused-over-limit`). Its public facts already say so per bounty: `pays_on_merge` is true only when "this bounty's reward equals the reward they approved for it there" (`src/feed.ts`).

Three problems follow:
1. **A change on one side silently breaks the other.** An owner who raises the reward from $10 to $20 with `config set reward 20` gets bounties the board will never charge. Nothing tells them: the tool holds no board session, so it cannot see the board's amount. The board's word for that amount is also "limit", while the tool's `reward-limit` means the most promised across open bounties. [BT89](BT89.md) resolves the name.
2. **The owner can be told to pay by hand when the board will charge.** At close, `payNotice` (`packages/box-bounty/pay.ts`) cannot see the switch. It says "If you turned on pay on merge for this repository on the board, the board charges your saved card instead", then goes on to "Pay `<solver>` at `<link>`". The board skips a charge only when the owner has *recorded* paying (`facts.recorded`). An owner who pays the solver outside the board and does not record it before the next minute's charge pays twice.
3. **An owner whose switch is on does not hear that a bounty is excluded.** A reward that differs, or a bounty posted before `on_at`, is excluded with no word to the owner. Only `discover` shows solvers "pays on merge: no".

**The owner's choice, 24 November 2026 (D-2026-11-24-charge-up-to-ceiling):** the board's approved amount becomes a **ceiling**, not an exact match. The reward then has one owner, the tool, locked at post. The board's value is only the owner's consent and its cap: one fact, one owner (D-2026-11-24-one-fact-one-owner). Raising a reward within the cap no longer stops charges, and a reward above it still waits for the owner.

## Outcome

0. **The charge rule becomes "up to".**
   - `decide` (`src/charge.ts`) charges a merged bounty its own reward (PF55's `posted_amount` where present, else the block's amount) when it is at least $1 and **at most** the approved amount.
   - Above the approved amount, it settles `refused-over-limit` as today, and the words say "above the amount you approved per bounty ($N)".
   - `pays_on_merge` (`src/feed.ts` and its schema description) means the same "at most".
   - The owner's page says "charges each merged bounty on this repository its own reward, up to $N".
   - The PF20 comment ("One fixed amount") is replaced with the new rule and its decision id.
1. **The tool reads the bounty's public `pays_on_merge`** (it already reads the board's detail, `readBountyDetail`) at three moments, and states it as a fact:
   - **`status <id>` after a post,** once the board lists the bounty: "pay on merge: applies to this bounty (the board charges your saved card at merge)" or "pay on merge: does not apply to this bounty". When the board's public facts show the repository has pay on merge on, it adds "its reward differs from the amount you approved on the board, or it was posted before you turned it on: `<board>/me/`".
   - **`close` after a merge:** when it applies, the pay message says "the board will charge your saved card for $X; do not also pay by hand. `omakase-box status <id>` shows the charge", and gives no hand-pay instruction. When it does not apply, the message is today's. When the board cannot be read, the message says so and asks the owner to check the bounty's page before paying by hand.
   - **`allow --reward` and `config set reward`:** one line, every time, when a board is set: "if pay on merge is on for `<repository>` on the board, set the same amount there, or its bounties are not charged: `<board>/me/`".
2. **The board shows the mismatch on the owner's page.** For each repository with pay on merge on, it says how many open bounties state a different reward and are therefore not charged, naming them. A fact, never a judgement.
3. **The board never charges after a hand payment the board was told about.** The bounty's page records "paid by hand" in one click, as today. The tool's pay message, when it says to pay by hand, also says to record it there or with `omakase-box config set paid <id>` before the next merge check, and explains why: otherwise pay on merge may charge as well.

## Traps

- **`pays_on_merge` is "as of" the board's last read** (its schema says so). The words say "the board says", never "will certainly".
- **The approved amount is the owner's private setting.** The tool must never show or ask for it, only the public yes or no, and point to the owner's page.
- **PF55 changes what "the bounty's reward" means** (the amount bound at post). Land it first, so both waves use one meaning.
