## Result

Run 19 November 2026 on the owner's go relayed through the Devin bot (TASK-12). Host: Devin, Linux VM; model not exposed to the session, so none is claimed.

**What changed.** `packages/box-bounty/publish-sink.ts` `checkOutbound` runs in `post.ts` immediately before the one first-publication push, for fresh posts and persisted retries alike, and the push sends the SHA it returns. It reads the destination itself (`ls-remote`), pins the commit and finds which advertised commits are held locally (one `cat-file --batch-check`), walks the commit minus those (`rev-list --parents`, every parent, so side histories and roots are in the walk), and reads every outbound commit's paths against its first parent or the empty tree (one `diff-tree --stdin`). Paths meet `shipRules(root)` read at the sink. A hit refuses `github-publish-never-ship`; any failed read refuses `github-git-failed`. Nothing is pushed or posted on either. The planner's `github-never-ship` is untouched. Docs: P2 and the refusal list in CONTRACTS.md, one paragraph in BOUNTY.md.
