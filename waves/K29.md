# K29: retire the older Box platform from master

Milestone: K (kitchen) · Station: packaging (also evidence) · Base: master at dispatch, after BT34 · Branch: `wave/K29` · Timebox: 240 minutes; at expiry the cook stops and reports where it is

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 18 October 2026. **The owner approved this removal on 18 October 2026** (PLAN.md, "The roadmap", Lean). Detailed for a cook without deep reasoning: an inventory first, then removal in chunks, each chunk green before the next.

## Why

The product is the bounty tool. The repository still carries the older Box platform it grew out of: about 21,000 lines in `services/`, `apps/` and nine `packages/`, the `box export`, `submit`, `verify` and `trace` commands, about fifteen test suites that every hosted run executes, an embedded Postgres that `verify` starts for them, docs, examples and about half of BACKLOG.md. After BT34 the bounty tool imports none of it. Every wave pays for it: in hosted-run time, in what an agent must read to understand the repository, and in noise.

## Outcome

Master holds the bounty tool, its tests, the kitchen, and nothing of the older platform; the hosted check runs only the bounty tool's suites and the kitchen's; `verify` no longer needs a database; and the whole older platform is preserved on an archive branch.

## Step 2: remove in chunks (150 minutes)

One commit per chunk, in this order, with `node scripts/workspace.mjs build` and the named suites green after each:

1. **The `box` command's older subcommands** (`export`, `submit`, `verify`, `trace`): remove them and their help lines; `box export` and the others now refuse by name, `command-removed`, "this command belonged to the older Box platform, retired on 18 October 2026; box bounty is the product" (the tool's rule: never fail silently). Then remove `tests/box/{package,trace,contracts,auth,delegation}`. Suites: `test:box-bounty`, `test:kitchen`.
2. **The older packages, apps, services, examples, infra** and their test folders. Suites: as above.
3. **Root wiring:** `package.json` scripts; development dependencies; `pnpm-workspace.yaml`; regenerate `pnpm-lock.yaml` with `node scripts/pnpm.mjs install` (the workspace's `minimumReleaseAge` applies; record any package the install changed beyond removals, and stop if one is added).
4. **Kitchen tooling:** `scripts/suite.mjs` (suite tables, `CHECK_SUITES`, `DB_SUITES`, `INERT`), `scripts/verify.mjs` (the `--db` modes and the embedded Postgres: remove them if no remaining suite needs a database, and say so), `scripts/workspace.mjs` (the check step's typecheck and suites); `tests/kitchen` tests that pin removed suites (`k12-db-suites`, `k6-*`, `verify-reuse` and others found by step 1): update each to the remaining suites, never weakening what it checks about them, and list every changed expectation with the reason "the suite it named no longer exists".
5. **Hosted check** (`.github/workflows/verify.yml`): the suite list becomes `test:box-bounty test:kitchen` (plus any kept suite); remove the Postgres hydration and other older-platform steps; keep `install-node22`, the pinned actions and `permissions: contents: read`.
6. **Docs and backlog:** remove the older-platform docs per step 1; README.md describes the bounty tool only; move BACKLOG.md's older-platform items into a single line pointing at the archive branch.
