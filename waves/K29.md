# K29: retire the older Box platform from master

Milestone: K (kitchen) · Station: packaging (also evidence) · Base: master at dispatch, after BT34 · Branch: `wave/K29` · Timebox: 240 minutes; at expiry the cook stops and reports where it is

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 18 October 2026. **The owner approved this removal on 18 October 2026** (PLAN.md, "The roadmap", Lean). Detailed for a cook without deep reasoning: an inventory first, then removal in chunks, each chunk green before the next.

## Why

The product is the bounty tool. The repository still carries the older Box platform it grew out of: about 21,000 lines in `services/`, `apps/` and nine `packages/`, the `box export`, `submit`, `verify` and `trace` commands, about fifteen test suites that every hosted run executes, an embedded Postgres that `verify` starts for them, docs, examples and about half of BACKLOG.md. After BT34 the bounty tool imports none of it. Every wave pays for it: in hosted-run time, in what an agent must read to understand the repository, and in noise.

## Outcome

Master holds the bounty tool, its tests, the kitchen, and nothing of the older platform; the hosted check runs only the bounty tool's suites and the kitchen's; `verify` no longer needs a database; and the whole older platform is preserved on an archive branch.
