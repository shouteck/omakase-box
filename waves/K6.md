## Outcome

Reduce repeated work in `test:box-package` without reducing its checks. Give cooks a scoped file-level inner loop, retain one full local acceptance run on the final commit, and ratchet an explicit executed-time budget so future waves cannot silently lose the gain. Master Actions is a safety net, not a run to wait for. Await the owner's pre-cook **go** before implementation.
