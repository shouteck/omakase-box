**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 3 November 2026, from the owner's review of `/me/` during PF18: "too unprofessional… the payment stuff… there is a way better way to present this… no structure at all." Awaits the owner's "go".

## Outcome

`/me/` reads like a product from a company known for its design: what is owed sits first, as figures and one table; owner things and solver things are apart; each way to be paid or to pay is a row with a status; account links live in a header menu. Every element names the big company's design system or screen it follows. Nothing about how payments work changes: same routes, same facts, same words of record.

## Build, in this order

1. **References first, recorded.** Read the public design systems and real screens of big companies praised for their design: GitHub Primer (layout, settings), Shopify Polaris (resource lists, badges, empty states), Stripe's dashboard patterns (status, empty state, sign-out, sending people to Stripe and back), Vercel and Linear (settings and billing screens, read on their own pages, not second-hand). Never Upwork or Algora. For each element below, one line under `## References` in this file: the element, the reference, what we take from it, cited.
2. **A mock-up, then the owner's approval, before any code.** Static, at desktop and phone width, light and dark, of the signed-in page in four states: a new person; an owner with a saved card and something owed; a solver connected with something owed to them; both roles at once. Sent as images. No code until the owner approves it; their words are quoted here.
3. **The structure** (to be confirmed by step 2):
   - **Money first:** "You owe" and "Owed to you" as two figures, then one table: bounty, person, amount, since, one action. Empty: a line that says why and the next step (Polaris' empty state).
   - **Two tabs, or a side list on desktop and a parent link on phones (Primer):** **Paying** (the three ways as status cards from `pay-ways.ts`, the saved card, the limit, the repositories) and **Getting paid** (one list of ways to be paid: Stripe, PayPal, each listed link; each a row with a status badge: Connected, Pending, Not connected, Not set up on this board).
   - **Adding a link:** an "Add a way to be paid" button and a small dialog, not a form left open; each row says who can see it.
   - **Account menu in the header:** your public pages and Sign out (Stripe's sign-out pattern), off the page's foot.
   - **Ways a board lacks** take no section of their own: one quiet line.
   - **Stripe's countries:** visible where a solver chooses, and readable signed out (How it works links here today and a signed-out reader sees nothing).
4. **Leftovers it fixes:** a paid bounty's page no longer says "Next: ask … how they want to be paid"; the bounty page's "Record $45 paid" matches the words the cards use (the owner chose "mark it paid" over "record" in PF18; confirm in step 2).
5. **Tests that read the real page:** each of the four states renders its sections and statuses from `/api/me`; a status that is made fixed fails; the header menu holds Sign out; no section names a way the board lacks; the money-claim check; keyboard reach of the tabs and the dialog. Show each failing once.
6. **Before-and-after full pages** (sticky menu static, reduced motion so nothing renders blank), four states, both widths and themes; the owner's approval quoted; then merge. The deploy needs its own word.

## References

Read on 3 November 2026, on the companies' own pages.

| Element | Reference | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| Page width, side list or tabs, phone | GitHub Primer, [layout](https://primer.style/product/getting-started/foundations/layout/) and [navigation](https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/navigation/) | At most 1280px; one column on phones without losing anything; tabs that only switch a panel are panels, not links; on a phone a detail drops its side list and gets a back link |
| Tabs, Paying and Getting paid | Primer, [UnderlinePanels](https://primer.style/product/components/underline-panels/guidelines/) | "Break related content into tabbed panels": underline tabs over panels on the same page |
| Empty states | [Stripe's empty state](https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/empty-state) and [Primer's](https://primer.style/product/ui-patterns/empty-states/) | Say why it is empty ("No … yet."), when data appears (under 14 words), and one action that answers the title; inside a section, a compact dashed box, not a page-sized one |
| Adding a way to be paid | Primer, [Dialog](https://primer.style/product/components/dialog/guidelines/) | A dialog with a title; primary action on the right of the footer; full screen on phones when it has inputs; the backdrop does not dismiss a form with unsaved changes |
| Account menu and sign-out | Vercel, [account management](https://vercel.com/docs/accounts) | The profile picture at the top right opens a menu holding settings; Stripe's own pattern for apps inside its dashboard puts sign-out below the content ([sign out](https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/sign-out)); this page follows Vercel, a site of its own |
| Status of each way | Stripe's [communicating state](https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns) and the dashboard's badges | One short status per row, in Stripe's words where Stripe decides it |
| Payments owed, a table | Stripe's [DataTable with an empty message](https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/patterns/empty-state) | A table of rows with one empty message, swapped by why it is empty |

Not reached on the day: Shopify Polaris (its pages refused the connection three times) and Linear's settings screens (its docs describe preferences, not the layout). Neither is cited.
