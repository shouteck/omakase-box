# PF58: fewer pages: one "how it works", the numbers on the front page, no RSS

Milestone: platform · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF58` · Timebox: 240 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-24-board-only-what-needs-a-website, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval
Horizon: medium: a look change across the site. Refactoring playbook; principles: subtract-before-you-add, experience-first.

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 24 November 2026 from the walkthrough of the board ("Sure. You can create the waves in the briefs"). It changes what the site looks like, so the design proposal starts from the real pages:
- patch the real pages;
- whole-page before-and-after screenshots;
- a reason for each change.

The owner approves those screenshots before anything merges. It is a removal of pages, so it also waits for the owner's "go".

**Why.** The site explains itself in five places that overlap:

| Page | Lines | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| `/` (the front page) | — | what the board is, the two ways in |
| `/how/` | 147 | how posting and solving work, the ways to pay |
| `/delegate/` | 35 | posting, again, for an owner |
| `/earn/` | 31 | solving, again, for a solver |
| `/agents/` | 66 | install, commands, the feed and MCP, for agents |

There is also `/numbers/`, a page of the board's totals that `/numbers.json` already serves to programs, and `/rss.xml`, which no agent needs: the JSON feed, MCP and `llms.txt` cover agents, and people use the board page.

Each page is one more thing to keep true and to approve when the words change.

## Outcome

1. **The pages that remain:**
   - `/`, the front page, which now also carries the numbers' headline line from `/numbers/`;
   - `/how/`, the one explanation, with four sections:
     - `#hand-off`: posting;
     - `#answer`: solving;
     - `#agents`: install, commands, the feed, MCP, `llms.txt`;
     - `#pay`: the three ways to pay, by hand, one click and on merge (D-2026-11-02-three-ways-to-pay);
   - `/board/`, `/b/<id>/`, `/me/`, `/owner/<login>/`, `/solver/<login>/`, `/sign-in/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`, all unchanged in this wave.
2. **Old addresses keep working,** each a `308` redirect:
   - `/delegate/` → `/how/#hand-off`;
   - `/earn/` → `/how/#answer`;
   - `/agents/` → `/how/#agents`;
   - `/numbers/` → `/#numbers`.

   The existing `/post` and `/docs` redirects point at the new places. `/numbers.json` and its schema stay, for programs.
3. **`/rss.xml` answers `410 Gone`** with one line: "RSS was retired; the newest bounties are at /bounties.json (filters, If-None-Match) and through MCP at /mcp."
4. **Every list of pages agrees:**
   - the top bar (`components/SiteNav.jsx`: Board, How it works, Your page);
   - `src/sitemap.ts`;
   - `src/llms.ts`;
   - the README's "For programs";
   - `docs/box-v4/PLATFORM-UX.md`.
5. **Nothing is lost,** checked by a test. Every heading and instruction on the removed pages appears on `/how/` or `/`, or is listed in the Result as dropped on purpose, with the reason.
