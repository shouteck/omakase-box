# PF10: the site looks and reads like a professional product

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `fc65a99` · Branch: `wave/PF10` · Timebox: 300 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-01-site-redesign, D-2026-11-01-real-before-launch, D-2026-10-21-no-platform-money
Horizon: long: agreed through `/plan`, kept below

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 1 November 2026, from the owner: the site still looks unprofessional and the front page's graphics seem wrong; think as a designer, from the eyes of someone visiting or using the flow. **Stops once for the owner's eye** before merging. Deploying needs the owner's separate word.

## Why

K43's rules and PF7's drawings produced a site that is calm but unfinished. The headline gives no reason to care, the drawings are metaphors a visitor has to decode, commands do not read as commands, caveats sit inside the steps, the hierarchy is flat, and the board shows a filter panel over nothing.

## Outcome

A visitor sees within one screen what the product is and why it matters, sees the real product rather than a metaphor, and can start from a command they can copy. Every page shares one finished system. No behaviour, data, API, feed, MCP or RSS changes.

## Plan

Agreed with the owner on 1 November 2026. Feature playbook, principles: experience-first, subtract-before-you-add, prove-it-works.

- **Second version, after the owner rejected the first (still unprofessional, too verbose; research Apple, Muji and current sites):** one idea per screen, few words, detail on `/how/` behind disclosures.
- **Rules (D-2026-11-01-site-redesign, PLATFORM-UX 7.2):** Geist Sans and Mono; sizes 13, 14, 16, 20, 28, 40, 56, 80; near-white ground, tiles, ink pill button; a soft sky and shadow behind the one picture; the picture tells its story once, off under reduced motion. One primary action and AA contrast stay.
- **Front page:** hero line "Work your agent can't finish, finished."; one product window in real text (`components/HeroWindow.jsx`), using the tool's real output, marked as an example; five tiles; four things it never does; two paths. `Parcel.jsx` is deleted. **`/how/`:** every step and command, and the fuller facts behind five disclosures.
- **Shell:** mark, favicon, navigation with current page and GitHub, footer, sharing metadata.
- **Board:** filters hidden when nothing is open; cards lead with title and reward. **Bounty:** summary panel beside the task; copyable branch; breadcrumb and not-found links go to `/board/`. **Other pages:** same system.
- **Tests:** expectations change only for sizes, families, drawings and motion. New checks: the example's output matches a real run; copy works; breadcrumb; empty board has no filters; no animation at all; new colour pairs meet AA.
