**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 11 November 2026, at the BT63 kickoff. Master's hosted run has been red since the BT64 merge (runs 42811930946 to 42922531052), always on one test: `tests/platform/pages.test.ts`, "your page: an owner sees what they owe…". It is not BT64's doing.

Mechanism, read from the code and the log: the fixtures are dated from a fixed clock (`NOW = 2026-10-31T12:00Z`, `tests/platform/support.ts:8`; `seedStates` makes the bounty 4 days old, so created 27 October). The age text, though, is worked out in the browser with the real clock (`ago()` in `packages/box-platform/components/Bounties.jsx:34`): "N days ago" up to 13 days, then "N weeks ago". The real date passed 10 November, 14 days after 27 October, so the page now correctly says "2 weeks ago" and the test still demands "N days ago". The received text in the log says exactly that. The test was green on 10 November at 02:07 and every run after the date passed has been red, which is also why a Linux-only reading of "flaky" is wrong: it fails on every system, on every run, from that date.
