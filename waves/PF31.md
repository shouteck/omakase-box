**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 4 November 2026, from the owner's review of a bounty page during PF9b ("verbose, information all over the place"; "asking the owner to mark as paid is just annoying") and the design agreed in that talk. Before PF32 and launch.

## Outcome

An owner who owes a solver pays from one small window, opened from Your page or the bounty page, and the payment is recorded without a separate step: a one-click payment by the provider's own word, a payment by hand by one "I've paid $X" button inside the window. The bounty page says what matters in a few lines, payment first among the facts, with nothing lost that the page states today.

## The payment window

- **One window, two ways in:** a "Pay" button on each "you owe" row of Your page and on the bounty page, for the owner only. Both open the same window. It has its own address (`/b/<id>/#pay`, the static export's way), so a link, an agent or a return from Stripe or PayPal can open it.
- **Inside, from the top:** whom and how much ("$50 to fern, for pull request #12, merged 3 November"), then the ways this solver can be paid, best first:
  - **One click** (Stripe, PayPal, whichever the solver connected): "Pay $50", on the provider's page, recorded automatically by the provider's webhook as today. The window says so before the click.
  - **By hand:** the solver's listed links and details shown in the window; under them one button, "I've paid $50", which records the payment (today's `paid` call) and closes the window. Only by hand has this button: a one-click payment never needs it.
  - **None listed:** says so, and what the solver can do (connect or list a way); nothing to record.
- **× closes without recording.** Nothing is recorded by opening, closing or following a link.
- **More than one merged solution** ("owner decides"): the window first asks whom, then shows that person's ways.
- **After:** the bounty page and Your page show "Paid through Stripe" or "Recorded paid by you", with the solver's "received" when they give it, as today. Withdrawing a record stays possible from the bounty page.
- **The solver's side is unchanged:** "Confirm I received" and "It has not arrived" stay on the bounty page; a solver's "received" still records.

## The bounty page

Keep every fact; change the arrangement and the words.

- **Keep:** the right-side summary card (reward, poster, repository, posted, solutions, payment ways, terms), the task with its attribution and issue link, how to answer while open, solutions, history.
- **The payment section becomes three lines at most** in the normal case: the state ("$50 owed to fern since 3 November" / "Paid through Stripe on 4 November" / "Recorded paid by mara on 4 November; fern confirmed it"), whose word each fact is, and the one action this visitor has (owner: Pay; solver: Confirm received). The longer explanations (the provider's fee, "recording moves no money", the self-dealt note, a disagreement) fold under one "Details" line, still on the page.
- **The CLI equivalence line** (`box bounty config set paid <id>`) moves to Details.
- **Word rules hold:** facts, never a verdict; a stranger's text never shown as the board's; every money claim names whose word it is (the existing money-claim check runs on the new screens).

## Plan

Written by the CTO at kickoff, 4 November 2026.

- **No API change is needed.** `/api/b/<id>/payment` already answers the role, whom (`payee`, `payees` with their methods), `can_pay`, `can_pay_paypal`, `can_record`, `can_withdraw`, `can_authenticate` and the facts; `paid` is already idempotent ("the same click twice changes nothing") and names whom. The window is front-end only, on `Dialog.jsx`'s pattern, fed by that answer. Your page's "you owe" rows need the bounty's id, already in `me.you_owe`.
- **Subtract first** (subtract-before-you-add): the panel's long sentences, the CLI line and the methods list leave the page's main column; the page keeps a three-line state, one action and Details. Then the window is added.
- **Order:** references (gate 1) → static mock-up in the site's tokens, sent to the owner (gate 2) → build only after the owner's words are quoted here → tests, each guard broken once → before-and-after shots for approval → verify → merge. The deploy is the owner's word.
- **Principles that set the target:** experience-first (one window, one primary action, nothing to record twice), model-the-domain (the window's content is a function of the payment answer's state: owner-decides → whom first; one click and by hand as listed ways; none listed), prove-it-works (an inventory test that every fact the old page stated is still on the new one).

## References

Gate 1, read by the CTO on 4 November 2026 from each company's own page. One line per element, the reference it follows.

| Element | Follows | What the reference says |
|---|---|---|
| A window, not a page, for paying | NN/g, "Modal and nonmodal dialogs" | modals are for "short, direct dialogs with the user" |
| Title names the task; × and Escape close it | GitHub Primer, Dialog | the title "also serves as the aria-label"; Escape or the X closes it |
| First line: how much, to whom | Apple, Human Interface Guidelines: Apple Pay | "Clarify the current payment amount in the total line" |
| Say the board is not the payee | Apple HIG: Apple Pay | when not the end merchant, "identify both businesses in the payment sheet" |
| One primary button; the provider's page takes the card | Stripe, hosted invoice page | customers view amounts and status, then pay with the enabled methods |
| The window has its own address | Stripe, hosted invoice page and Payment Links | each invoice gets a unique URL; a link anyone can be sent |
| By hand: the solver's details shown before "I've paid" | GitHub Sponsors | payment details are reviewed in their sections before finishing |
| After paying: says it went through and what happens next | GOV.UK Design System, confirmation pages; Apple HIG | tells users the transaction went through and what happens next; "Display an order confirmation" |
| Bounty page: the state first, the long words folded | GitHub Primer, progressive disclosure | use it "only when it's necessary to truncate information", to keep the view compact |

Not read: Wise, PayPal, Revolut, Cash App and Shopify's guidance pages did not return readable text to the CTO's fetch, so none is cited. Upwork and Algora were not consulted.

What the owner settled, in order:
- **The bounty page:** the left "Payment for this bounty" section goes. The top of the right-hand card holds the reward and the state; when the viewer has something to do it is one dark tile ("Owed to example-quill · $45 · Pay →"), otherwise the reward with one status line. The long explanations move under "Payment details and terms".
- **One way to GitHub:** "Open the bounty branch on GitHub" is removed (How to answer names the branch); one outlined "View the issue on GitHub ↗" button sits in the card on every bounty. "How to answer" shows while the bounty is open, to everyone, and goes once it is merged or closed.
- **Stripe's two cases are told apart:** "Paid on merge through Stripe" (nothing to click) and "Paid through Stripe" (the owner's one click).
- **The solver only confirms:** "Confirm I received $X", on the bounty and on the solver's Your page, where a recorded payment now stays until confirmed. There is no "it has not arrived"; until confirmed the bounty says "not yet confirmed".
- **Your page:** today's tables unchanged; the amount becomes the button ("Pay $45", "Confirm $45"), left-aligned like every other column.
- **The window** (approved in every state): one click, by hand, none listed, back from the provider, after "I've paid". When more than one solution is merged, it asks whom first, listing only the people and pull requests; the next step shows every way that person can be paid.
- **The human way** (choose whom to pay, merged or not, one paid) is PF32's.
