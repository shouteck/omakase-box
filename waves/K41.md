**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 20 October 2026. The owner asked for a promotional video like those that accompany recently shipped agent tools, and gave two examples: Shipper's `epstein.md` launch (26 s) and Treg's Signal Monitor (64 s), both on X. **Runs after BT41**, so every frame shows the final text. Cook: high (taste and pacing are the work); reviewer: medium.

## What the examples do (CTO's frame-by-frame read)

- **Short:** 26 s and 64 s. One idea per beat, about two to four seconds each.
- **Works with the sound off:** X autoplays muted, so the meaning is carried by large on-screen text, a few words at a time (kinetic type).
- **Plain and calm:** a light, near-white background with one accent colour, and a dark end card.
- **Real product footage between the words:** Treg shows its actual results table and the sheet it fills. The words name each step ("treg monitors… any buyer signal", "Putting hot leads in Google Sheet…").
- **One concrete result:** Treg's "Shortlisted 38 high-potential leads for $0.98".
- **Ends on name plus one action:** the logo and a single line telling the viewer what to do.

## Outcome

A 40 to 60 second MP4 (1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264) that shows how box bounty works, end to end, with real footage. It is built from code so it can be re-rendered when the tool's text changes, and handed to the owner to post or not.

## Rules

1. **Every frame of tool output is real.** Capture it from a real run: K40's sandbox run, or a fresh run on `shouteck/box-bounty-sandbox` at $0 under D-2026-10-19-k31-sandbox-go, closed afterwards. Typing animation may reveal the exact captured text. Lines may be trimmed (keep whole lines) but never edited. Store the raw captures beside the project, and have the reviewer diff every terminal frame against them.
2. **No claim the tool does not back.** No numbers that were not measured; no "thousands of solvers". Nothing may suggest solvers are already waiting: the tool itself says "Nobody is known to be watching these issues yet". Showing one solver answering the demo bounty is fine, since it really happened. Every caption must be true by the README or a test.
3. **The end card's install line.** Use `npx box-bounty bounty help` only if the owner has published (or decided) the name by the time of render. Otherwise show the repository URL `github.com/shouteck/omakase-box`. Make the line one prop so it is a one-line re-render.
4. **Sound off by design.** No voiceover. No music in the render, because licensed music is the owner's choice; leave a clean place for an audio track.
5. **Look.** A near-white background, one accent colour, a clean sans for words and a monospaced font for the terminal (open licences only, recorded with their licence). No emojis and no gradients. The GitHub screenshots are of the public sandbox, cropped to the issue or pull request, with no browser chrome showing other tabs or accounts.
6. **Rebuildable.** A [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev) project (React components rendered to MP4) in `kitchen/launch/video/`, with its own `package.json`, outside the pnpm workspace and outside `verify`. `README.md` there gives the one render command. Confirm Remotion's licence terms from its own licence page and record them in the Result: they depend on company size, and the owner needs the fact.
