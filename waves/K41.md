## What the research found

- **Claude Haiku 5.5 launch (11 s):**
  - Warm ivory ground; three small tiles cut into notched block shapes, each filled with a rich texture (fabric, sky, sunset, microscopy).
  - One thin dotted arc crosses the frame, and the name rises along it in a serif.
  - No interface and no claims: restraint and texture carry it.
- **Claude Dashboards and Motion launch (81 s):** a serif title card with an italic accent word; a soft two-colour palette; real product screens in rounded frames, driven by a cursor; ends on the mark alone.
- **Anthropic's stated brand principles** (Studio Geist, Everett): warmth over modernity, accessibility over exclusivity, thoughtful restraint over flashy spectacle.
- **Launch-film craft in general:** under 60 s; a hook in the first 4 s; the core loop shown faster than real time; ending on the name and one action; sound carries much of the feel.

## Outcome

Two silent-first films built from code, sharing one set of parts:

- **The film** (about 55 s): how box bounty works, told as a parcel of work travelling out and answers coming back, ending on the use cases it unlocks.
- **The teaser** (10–15 s): a mood piece with no interface.

Both render at 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4. They are rebuildable when the tool's text changes and handed to the owner to post or not.

## The visual language (our own; take Claude's principles, never its identity)

**Minimalism first** (owner, 21 October 2026): fewer elements, more space, one idea per frame. Paper and ink stays; it leans Muji (natural, unbleached, restrained). It must share the platform's ground, accent and type (K43), so the film and the board read as one brand.

1. **Ground:** warm ivory paper with a subtle grain, generated in code (noise or shader), not a photo.
2. **Ink:** near-black lines that draw themselves (stroke reveal), with a slight hand wobble (displacement on the stroke). Shapes are drawn, not boxed in UI chrome.
3. **One recurring shape, the parcel:** a small folded paper box tied with string. It is the bounty: the task packed up. It travels along **one dotted path** and returns. Every scene uses the parcel or the path.
4. **Type:**
   - **Inter for everything** (owner, D-2026-10-22-k43-decisions): words on screen and the tool's own typeset text, in the board's two weights. It is the same family as the platform, so the film and the board read as one brand.
   - All fonts open-licence (SIL OFL), with licences recorded.
   - Tool output is **typeset like a printed page**, not shown as a terminal window.
5. **Colour:** ivory, ink, and **one** accent of our own. The accent must be outside the warm terracotta range Claude uses (hue roughly 10–25°, for example `#C15F3C`, `#C96442`, `#D97757`). The accent is **ink blue `#234a7d`** (owner, D-2026-10-22-k43-decisions), shared with the board, on its unbleached-paper ground `#f5f2ea` with ink `#1d1b17`.
6. **Never:**
   - Claude's spark mark, Anthropic's typefaces (Styrene, Tiempos), "Claude" or "Anthropic" anywhere on screen, or any frame that could pass for an official Claude release.
   - Emojis, gradients or glow.
   - Stock imagery, unless it is CC0 with the source recorded.

**The punchline's honesty rule.** The five lines are reasons agents can hand work off, taken from `describe` and K44's hand-off table, written as possibilities, never as claims that agents already do. ## Rules

1. **The tool's words are real.** Every line of tool output, issue text and pull-request title comes from a real run: K40's sandbox run, or a fresh $0 run on `shouteck/box-bounty-sandbox` under D-2026-10-19-k31-sandbox-go, closed afterwards. Store the raw captures beside the project. Text may be trimmed (whole lines) and typeset, never reworded. The reviewer diffs every text frame against the captures.
2. **No claim the tool does not back.**
   - No measured-sounding numbers.
   - Nothing implying solvers are already waiting: the tool itself says nobody is known to be watching. Scene 4's "Posted where anyone can answer" is the limit.
   - Every caption maps to a README line or a test, in a table in the Result.
3. **The install line** is `npx box-bounty bounty help` only if the owner has published or fixed the name by render time. Otherwise it is the repository address. Make it one prop.
4. **Silent-first.**
   - Every meaning is carried on screen.
   - No music in the render, because licensed music is the owner's choice and may cost money.
   - Part 2 may add a separate, optional track of subtle sounds generated in code (paper, a click, a soft landing), with no samples and no licence, kept on its own so the owner can drop it.
5. **Built from code.** Use a [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev) project in `kitchen/launch/video/`, with its own `package.json`, outside the pnpm workspace and `verify`. Record Remotion's licence terms from its own licence page; they depend on company size, and the owner needs the fact. If they do not fit, use plain HTML, SVG and canvas rendered frame by frame with Playwright and encoded with ffmpeg instead, and say why.
