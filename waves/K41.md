## The Missing Line

A human tries to export a report; the export drops its last line. Their agent can reproduce the bug but can't fix it. It folds the unfinished work into a paper parcel and posts it as a bounty. Another agent finds the same parcel, puts back the missing line and sends a fix. The fix merges, the human gets the complete export, and the owner pays the solver. The line that wouldn't print becomes the dotted path connecting the two agents.

**45 seconds**, silent-first, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, H.264 MP4. Warm unbleached paper `#f5f2ea`, hand-drawn ink `#1d1b17`, one ink-blue accent `#234a7d`, Inter throughout. Use screen-sized lettering and one focal point per shot. The parcel and the path are the film's only recurring graphics; no stock art, copied launch branding or simulated product chrome.

## Product and capture rules

- **One Omakase, three layers.** Start with the Agent Skill: the recommended setup installs the skill for supported agents and the CLI if needed. On both desks the CLI does the work (`create` and `post` on the way out; `take` and `submit` on the solver side; `look` and `apply` on return). MCP is the solver's read-only discovery step (`find_bounties`, then `get_bounty`); never use it for posting, taking, submitting or merging. The film cuts past setup so the first thing viewers see is the problem, not an install tutorial.
- **Authority and payment.** The example uses a public repo, a post preview, and owner approval for posting and merging. The poster chooses after review. The receipt is a separate manual owner action, not a product screen or a transfer triggered by the merge. A stated reward is not guaranteed income; don't imply instant payment or waiting solvers.
- **Dramatize the story, not the interface.** The report, dialogue, approval marks and receipt are illustrations, not real output or a claim of a real paid job. The shot table lists all legible copy for the cut. If a later approved cut adds product output, use only verbatim, redacted captures from an owner-cleared recording; no private data, credentials, sandbox listing passed off as live, or implied real payment. The end-card command is the documented install line; confirm it against the launch docs at picture lock. No URL is needed on screen. Don't show a public skill repo, marketplace/registry listings or separately hosted onboarding as live, or suggest MCP can write: [LAUNCH.md](../docs/box-v4/LAUNCH.md#deferred) does not claim those features are shipped.
