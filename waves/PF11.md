## Outcome

A solver can connect their own Stripe account. An owner can save a card, set a per-bounty limit and turn on pay on merge per repository, on the signed-in board page. When exactly one eligible solution lands, the board starts a direct charge to the merged author's own account and never holds money. Every attempt ends in a named state. Solvers without Stripe get clean provider tiles. Every bounty, the feed and MCP state "agents welcome" and the first facts for judging it.

Mechanism and limit: the limit and the payee are enforced by this code, not by Stripe. Whoever holds the platform's key can bypass both (K46).
