# PF45: the front page's sections are never blank before they are scrolled to

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Branch: `wave/PF45`

Owner decisions: D-2026-11-11-site-settled, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval. Asked for by the owner's website-quality Bot on 19 November 2026 after [PF43](PF43.md) left the shared motion alone. Not deployed: deploying is the owner's word.

## Outcome

"From stuck to merged", "Some things it never does" and the two closing cards are readable in a full-page capture and in a print of the front page, wherever the reader has scrolled. Sections still rise 12px as they arrive; they no longer fade from `opacity: 0`.

## Evidence before

Live front page, 19 November 2026, Chrome 156, unscrolled at 1440×900 and 390×844: every `.arrive` element computed `opacity: 0` in a full-page screenshot, with JavaScript off (the reveal is CSS only, `animation-timeline: view()`), and under print media. With reduced motion, after a scroll, after a `#never` anchor jump, and scrolling with the CPU throttled 6×, all were `opacity: 1`. So a reader scrolling on screen sees every section; one who prints or saves the page as a PDF from the top, or any full-page capture, gets blank space.
