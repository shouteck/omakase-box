# PF40: one site: a mark, a navigation with one action, one grid on every page

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF40` · Timebox: 300 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-10-one-name, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval, D-2026-11-02-design-references-big-companies, D-2026-10-21-minimalism, D-2026-11-01-site-redesign
Horizon: long: start with `/plan` and keep the plan in this file. **Stops twice for the owner:** once for the mock-ups, once before deploy.

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K56](K56.md). The front page and the rest of the site look like two products; the site has no mark and three names; the navigation has no action and loses a link on a phone; the footer is one line. This wave builds the frame every later design wave stands in. It changes no page's content.

## Outcome

1. **A mark and the name,** top left on every page, and as the browser icon. **The name is omakase-box** (owner, 10 November 2026, D-2026-11-10-one-name): the site's title, the mark, the browser tab, the page titles and the footer's "run by" line all say it; "the board" stays the name of one page. The mock-ups show each place the old title appears today and what replaces it, found by search.
2. **A navigation with one action:** the links in plain ink, one filled button at the right end ("Get started", leading where it does today), the page you are on marked. On a phone every link is reachable.
3. **One grid:** the same outer margins, column width and vertical rhythm on every page, taken from the front page. Inner pages stop hugging the left edge with half the screen empty.
4. **One page header:** title, one sentence, and at most one link, set the same way on every page that is not the front page.
5. **A footer that holds its links** in two or three short groups, with the one line about money and who runs the board.

## Done when

- Accepted as the rulebook then says ([K50](K50.md) if merged), with `test:platform` and `test:kitchen`.
- **Read first, cited in the Result:** how four or more sites praised for design set their mark, navigation and grid, looked at on the day at real desktop and phone sizes, never Upwork or Algora; and current accessibility guidance for navigation on small screens.
- **The mark: three candidates,** each simple enough to draw in one colour at 16 pixels, shown on the navigation, the browser tab and a dark ground. No drawing tool's stock shapes, no letter in a rounded square. The owner picks one or asks for more.
- **The mock-up stop:** static mock-ups of the frame on four pages (front, the board, For agents, Your page signed out), as full-page before-and-after screenshots at 1440 and 390 wide, light and dark, the sticky menu static. Shown as a walk: the page before and the page after in a visitor's path, side by side. The owner's approval quoted before any page is built.
- After building: every public and signed-in page adopts the frame; a test fails if a page renders without the shared header, navigation, footer or frame, or with more than one filled button in the navigation.
- Contrast recomputed for anything new and stated (WCAG 2.2 AA); the focus ring and "Skip to content" still first; the navigation works by keyboard at 390 wide.
- No layout shift at load: the navigation and header occupy their final space at first paint, checked in a browser with the network slowed.
- The signed-in pages are walked once in a real browser after the change; nothing but the frame moved.
- Deploying is the owner's word; `exceededCpu` before and after.
