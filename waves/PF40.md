# PF40: one site: a mark, a navigation with one action, one grid on every page

Milestone: PF (the platform) · Station: packaging · Base: `master at dispatch` · Branch: `wave/PF40` · Timebox: 300 minutes; at expiry, report partial findings and stop
Owner decisions: D-2026-11-10-one-name, D-2026-11-02-site-looks-need-approval, D-2026-11-02-design-references-big-companies, D-2026-10-21-minimalism, D-2026-11-01-site-redesign

**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K56](K56.md), and rewritten the same day so that any model can cook it: the design is already drawn. The front page and the rest of the site look like two products; the site has no mark and three names; the navigation has no action and loses a link on a phone; the footer is one line. This wave builds the frame every later design wave stands in. It changes no page's content.

**The picture to build to** is in `docs/box-v4/design/`. Read its `README.md` first, then run `node docs/box-v4/design/shoot.mjs --before` and look at every file in `docs/box-v4/design/shots/`. You are building the navigation, page header and footer that every mock-up there shows. You are not building the board, the reading pages or the hero: those are PF41, PF42 and PF43.

## Outcome

1. **A mark and the name,** top left on every page, and as the browser icon. **The name is omakase-box** (owner, 10 November 2026): the site's title, the mark, the browser tab, the page titles and the footer all say it; "the board" stays the name of one page.
2. **A navigation with one action:** the links in plain ink, one filled button at the right end ("Get started", leading to `/how/` as the front page's button does today), the page you are on marked. On a phone every link is reachable.
3. **One grid:** the same outer margins and width on every page, taken from the front page. Inner pages stop hugging the left edge.
4. **One page header:** title, one sentence, and at most one link, set the same way on every page that is not the front page.
5. **A footer that holds its links** in three short lists, with the one line about money and who runs the board.
