## Outcome

On every page:

1. The first link in the top bar shows **mark A and the name `omakase-box`** in place of the words "Open bounty board".
2. The bar ends with **one filled button, "Get started"**, leading to `/how/`.
3. **On a phone every link is reachable:** the name and the button on the first row; Board, How it works, For agents and Your page on the second. The GitHub link leaves the bar on a phone only (it is in the footer).
4. The browser tab's icon is mark A, and page titles say `omakase-box`.

Nothing else on any page changes: not the width, not the footer, not a heading.
