**Brief.** Written by the CTO on 10 November 2026 from [K49](K49.md). Three facts:

- The full verify takes about 5 minutes on GitHub's Linux machine and 20 to 31 minutes on this host, where it also times out under load (PF33: three runs; BT44: four).

- `pnpm audit --prod` shows 2 high advisories through `zod`, which `packages/box-cli/package.json` lists and nothing imports.

The repository is public, so hosted minutes are free on all three systems.

## Outcome

Pushing a `wave/*` branch runs the full verify and the platform suite on Linux, Windows and macOS, and the packed package's install test on all three. **The hosted run on the wave's commit is the acceptance run** (owner, 10 November 2026: the local run "is actually taking out a lot of our development time"); local runs are for the files just touched. The production dependency audit is clean and stays checked.
