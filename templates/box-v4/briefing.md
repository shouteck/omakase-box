# Wave briefing

Wave ID and immutable brief revision/hash. Plan version and exact candidate/base. Proposed task IDs and dependencies. Approval status is pending until actual human approval is recorded.

## What the CEO is deciding

Desired user outcome, what this wave will serve, what it will not add, and the CTO's recommendation. Show a short before/after and explicit decisions; avoid implementation jargon in the main briefing.

## Station reports

| Station | Reuse/current evidence | Proposed workers and outcome | Independent verification | Risks/blockers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product and Experience | Actual paths/receipts | Actual IDs or proposed roles | Reviewer and observable user behavior | Decisions |
| Platform and Execution | Interfaces and current capability | Actual IDs or proposed roles | Exact artifact/runtime checks | Placement/Git/integration |
| Assurance and Research | Existing review and gaps | Actual IDs or proposed roles | Acceptance/provenance/claim audit | Missing evidence |

## Approval scope

Task IDs, allowed and forbidden paths, dependencies, baseline, runtime and local/cloud placement, available models, concurrency and heavy-check limits, existing-resource/spending limit, exact verification and timebox. Explicitly list whether product writes, plugin setup, model configuration, process startup, local commits/merges, remote push/PR or other external actions are allowed. Default absent actions are not authorized.

## Stop and resume

Scope/capability changes that require a new briefing; hold/revocation behavior; candidate/evidence handoff; what remains after this wave. Completion permits a next briefing, not its automatic execution.

## Evidence pointers

Immutable brief, graph definitions, receipts/known gaps and actual participating agents. Ask the CEO to approve or amend this named wave in their own words. No special reply token is needed.
