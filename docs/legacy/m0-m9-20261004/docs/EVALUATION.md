# Research instruments and later pilot

## Two questions, separate from software completion

H1 asks whether precise independently executable tasks retain value for expert engineers when autonomous AI is unreliable under stated budgets. H2 asks how much valuable frontend, backend, security, cloud and other work can be abstracted with acceptable disclosure, fit and preparation cost. P versus NP is motivation only; no complexity result is needed or claimed.

Agents implement the measurement system and study materials by M9. They cannot recruit, pay or impersonate participants under this build instruction. Synthetic fixtures exercise the instrument only. M10 is an optional later research programme, pending real customer and participant authorization; it is not a prerequisite to build the MVP or evidence that the hypotheses are true.

## Dataset and records

Define `CandidateRequirement` with candidate ID, repository/workspace cohort, pre-change revision, nonexclusive disciplines, adapter set, work-unit count and interfaces, PM-approved goal, intended value/effort estimate, evidence availability and disclosure policy. Log every candidate before selection. Preparation records include method, internal minutes by PM/engineer, retained private responsibilities, rejection reason, generated versions, attempted release, contract/check hashes and assurance level.

`ExecutionRecord` includes package hash, assigned executor category, expertise/agent version, allowed AI assistance, declared time/compute budget, cold-start outcome, clarification/defect reports, public/private checks, accepted outcome, rework, review/integration minutes, costs entered by the owner and context isolation confirmation. Human completion is human-reported/reviewed evidence, never an agent-generated event. Record missing values as null with a reason, not zero.

## Comparisons and metrics

Compare manual packages, AI packaging existing boundaries, and AI creation of checked boundaries while holding executor type comparable. Separately compare expert workflows, including permitted AI assistance, with autonomous agents on the same released information and declared budgets. Keep direct private-AI delivery as an additional reference. Prevent reference-fix/context leakage and repeat tasks only with an explicit contamination protocol.

Report candidate denominator, eligible/accepted fractions by discipline, independent no-clarification completion, private transfer, package defects, rejection reasons, retained private work, assurance levels and total preparation/review/integration effort. Weight breadth by estimated effort/value as well as task count. Track security/cloud/data/tooling assessment records individually. The MVP includes an executable bounded security-policy adapter; other requirements may still lack a suitable adapter. `not_supported` is a real result. Discipline totals overlap for composed tasks; report unique task denominators and composition cohorts without double-counting.

The initial 30-60-task study in the PDF is exploratory, not a sample-size guarantee. Stratify by discipline and difficulty, account for repository/requester/contributor clustering, show uncertainty and avoid a headline human/AI win from selected cases. Measure criticality and quality alongside completion. Zero observed leaks in this sample establishes no rare-failure guarantee.

## Gates to recommend, not fabricate

Before an actual private pilot, require the real isolated runner, approved minimal data, a configured private provider, independent security review and frozen participant/payment terms. Before a usefulness claim, require real PM/engineer effort and independent completions on valuable tasks. Before breadth claims, require evidence from distinct requirement families/customer systems. The report generator must print `insufficient_evidence` when inputs lack these records.

Create an empty versioned study CSV/JSON template, a populated clearly synthetic fixture set, a neutral protocol and a CLI report/export. Empty or synthetic-only data must produce an explicit unevaluated-hypotheses report. No UI should say experts outperform AI or universal abstraction is achieved based on seeded demo records.

## Composition evidence

Measure single-unit, full-stack and broader codebase compositions separately. Record interface/end-to-end failures, combined disclosure decisions, retained work and total preparation/integration effort. M9 synthetic full-stack and policy examples validate those implemented pathways only; they do not establish general frontend/backend/security/cloud coverage or expert-human advantage.
