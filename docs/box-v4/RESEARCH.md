# Validation and later research

Follow [the delegation validation plan](delegation/VALIDATION-PLAN.md). The initial commercial hypothesis is engineering teams repeatedly delegating bounded changes to contractors they choose. Define ordinary delegation comparisons in D1; measure preparation, onboarding and acceptance throughout D2-D3; separate D4 capability from effort, actual repeat use and commercial evidence. Competitor overlap is documented there. A self-service product or synthetic pass is not proof of differentiation. Prepare pilot instruments without starting unauthorised outreach, private-data access or payments.

The CEO's 5 October refocus prioritises the portable delegation experiment in [DELEGATION-MAP.md](DELEGATION-MAP.md) and the D4 proof. Measure the entire owner-preparation/recipient-work/independent-verification/integration loop, including failed packages and rework. PM chat and visual preview quality support that loop; they are not the sole value proposition. First demonstrate selected-source packaging for supported trusted non-sensitive tasks. Restricted semantic extraction/parameterisation, hostile execution and bounty settlement remain separate later research, not implied by exporting an ordinary source package. No novelty or customer demand is inferred from a narrower roadmap.

The software roadmap covers the supplement's stages 0-3. Measure preparation time, approval time, missing context, visible-check false confidence, independent acceptance, integration/rework, context size, preference and repeat use. Include attempted/rejected/failed Boxes in denominators. Split human, agent and hybrid records and overlapping disciplines without double-counting unique tasks.

Store clearly synthetic examples and empty study templates first. Real participant recruitment, messaging, payment and private customer data require separately authorized pilots. A UI walkthrough is not independent human completion. Demo responses cannot establish intent completeness; a live model producing valid JSON cannot establish useful Boxes.

The private intent-model service remains company-controlled. MVP retrieval supplies relevant pinned code/evidence to an existing model; custom training is not a prerequisite. Keep demo and local-model modes distinct, record endpoint/model/configuration evidence, and benchmark behavior/unknowns/task quality before a usefulness claim. pstack models build this product; they are not the customer's private intent model. Do not route private inference through developer cloud agents by accident.

## Later stages remain gated

| Stage | Candidate later work | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Restricted proprietary context and isolated private acceptance | Explicit release policy, independent completion, unchanged dedicated hostile-runner controls and measured useful coverage |
| 5 | Extraction/parameterization/simulators for selected task classes | Contract satisfaction plus private fit, counterexamples, drift/preservation and stated assurance limits |
| 6 | Vetted external execution | Frozen independently runnable Box, bounded feedback, total effort/rework and controlled transfer |
| 7 | Optional bounty routing | Matching/reservation/payment/dispute policy after Box preparation has measured value |

Legacy source/research and implementation adapters remain assets for these stages. None is implementation-eligible under B8 by default. Full semantic confidentiality, universal slicing and automatic correct intent are open research, not software checkboxes. Well-defined work can remain difficult; expert-human value versus autonomous agents is empirical, not a P versus NP theorem.

Promote the roadmap by evidence and explicit CEO waves, not elapsed weeks. Early software may proceed while these hypotheses remain unverified, provided its claims and trust profile are honest.
