Kitchen-wide lessons and ideas, one line each, ten at most. Only the head chef writes here. Emptied at every kickoff: each line is adopted or dropped. See "Getting better" in docs/box-v4/KITCHEN.md.
- A blind solver starts in an empty folder outside every checkout, never in the repository; R3b-2's solver started here with wave 1's evidence one file away.

- A brief that says "stop on a product refusal" must also say who may approve a workaround; the R3b-2 cook worked round one before asking.
